# Privacy Policy

**Wasp Inc.**

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

Wasp Inc. ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (https://wasp.sh).

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the site.

---

## What Data Do We Collect?

Wasp Inc. collects the following types of personal data:

- IP address
- the country your visit comes from
- browser type, operating system and device type
- the website or link that sent you to us
- pages visited
- time spent on pages
- links and buttons you click, for example a copy of the install command
- your email address, if you sign up for our newsletter

## How Do We Collect Your Data?

We collect most of this data automatically when you visit our website, with the analytics tools in [Analytics and Cookie Consent](#analytics-and-cookie-consent). You give us your email address when you sign up for our newsletter.

## How Will We Use Your Data?

Wasp Inc. collects your data so that we can:

- Analyze website traffic
- Improve our website
- Understand how visitors use our site

## Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to help us operate our website, conduct our business, and provide services to you. These services may collect information about your visit to our website.

We use the following third-party services:

- **Plausible Analytics**: website analytics without cookies. Plausible stores visitor data in the EU.
- **PostHog**: website analytics with cookies. PostHog stores the data in the United States (PostHog Cloud US).
- **Cloudflare**: hosting for our website.
- **Loops**: our newsletter. Loops receives your email address when you sign up.
- **Algolia**: the search in our documentation. Algolia receives what you type in the search box.
- **YouTube**: videos in some of our blog posts. YouTube can set its own cookies when a page with a video loads.
- **Google Fonts**: some fonts on our website. Your browser downloads them from Google.
- **GitHub (Giscus)**: comments on some documentation pages. The comments are GitHub Discussions in our GitHub repository.

These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use your information. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

## Analytics and Cookie Consent

We use analytics to learn how people find and use our website, so that we can improve it. We use two tools:

- **Plausible Analytics** counts every visit. It sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. It counts visitors with an ID that changes every day, so it cannot recognize you on a later day.
- **PostHog** sets a cookie, so it can recognize your browser on a later visit. It records the pages you visit and the links and buttons you click.

Whether PostHog runs depends on where you visit from:

- **In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, we show a cookie banner. PostHog starts only after you click "Accept all". If you click "Reject all", PostHog does not run.
- **Everywhere else**, PostHog starts when you open our website, and we do not show a cookie banner.

To decide this, our server checks which country your request comes from. We do not store this check. If we cannot tell the country, we show the cookie banner.

You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page. "Reject all" stops PostHog and deletes its cookie. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, PostHog does not record your visit.

We only share information with these parties as necessary to provide our services to you, and they are obligated to keep your information confidential and secure.

## Cookies

Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard Internet log information and visitor behavior information. When you visit our website, we may collect information from you automatically through cookies or similar technology.

For further information, visit [allaboutcookies.org](https://allaboutcookies.org).

### How Do We Use Cookies?

Our website sets these cookies:

- **`wasp-cookies-consent`** (functional): remembers your choice in the cookie banner. It lasts one year.
- **`ph_<project key>_posthog`** (analytics): set by PostHog, to recognize your browser on a later visit. It lasts one year. PostHog also keeps a copy of this data in your browser's local storage. In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, PostHog sets it only after you click "Accept all".

Third-party content, such as YouTube videos, can set its own cookies.

### Managing Cookies

You can change your choice for PostHog at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.

You can also set your browser not to accept cookies, and the website [allaboutcookies.org](https://allaboutcookies.org) tells you how to remove cookies from your browser. However, in a few cases, some of our website features may not function as a result.
